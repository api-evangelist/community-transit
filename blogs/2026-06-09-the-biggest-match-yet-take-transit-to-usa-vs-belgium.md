Community Transit offers multiple transit options for attending the USA vs. Belgium soccer match at Seattle Stadium, including Link light rail from Lynnwood City Center Station and Sounder train service from Everett, Mukilteo, and Edmonds. The agency also provides information about local watch parties and fare payment options to help residents travel to tournament events throughout Snohomish County.
