Community Transit is promoting transit access to World Cup 2026 watch parties and events throughout Snohomish County, along with a "Great Soccer Ball Hunt" scavenger hunt featuring five artist-designed buoys. Riders can visit these locations via transit, collect passport stamps, and claim prize bags at the Ride Store.
